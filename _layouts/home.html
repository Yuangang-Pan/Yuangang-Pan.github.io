I am a Professor at the Shenzhen Institute for Advanced Study, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). I am a member of the Data Intelligence Group (Diggers), led by Prof. Lixin Duan. Prior to joining UESTC in Sep 2026, I was a PI and Senior Research Scientist at the Centre for Frontier AI Research (CFAR), Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR), Singapore. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the University of Technology Sydney (UTS), Australia, in March 2020, under the supervision of Prof. Ivor Tsang.

My research interests include Generative AI and AI for Science & Healthcare, with a particular focus on foundation models, AI alignment, and AI-driven biomolecular design.

🔥🔥🔥 We are recruiting motivated researchers and students to join our group at UESTC (Shenzhen)!

Fully funded opportunities are available for Master's and Ph.D. students, postdoctoral researchers, associate research fellows, and other research positions. We also welcome visiting students and research interns.

Our current research directions include:

  • Pluralistic alignment of Large Language Models and Diffusion Models.
  • RNA foundation models, RNA 3D structure prediction and evaluation.
  • Protein foundation models, protein design, antibody sequence-structure co-design, AI agents, and AI-powered next-generation enzyme engineering.

For suitable students, internship opportunities at CFAR A*STAR Singapore may also be available through our ongoing collaborations, under the supervision of my collaborators at CFAR.

If you are interested in joining or collaborating with our group, please feel free to contact me with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.
